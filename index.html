About Me

I started writing software because I wanted to build tools that solve genuine day-to-day hassles rather than just following textbook exercises. Over my engineering degree at Anand Engineering College in Agra, that curiosity pulled me deep into modern web development—focusing on React and TypeScript for responsive user interfaces, and Node.js, Express, and FastAPI on the backend.

During my 4-week industrial MERN internship at the Academy of Skill Development (where I was awarded Grade A++), I built the Smart Job Portal. That project was my turning point: it proved to me how powerful software becomes when you actually understand the domain you're building for.

People often ask why I completed a BBA and enrolled in an MBA in HR (both through distance education) while studying computer science. The answer is simple: the hardest engineering challenges aren't just algorithmic—they're about understanding people, hiring funnels, team dynamics, and business workflows. Studying organizational behavior, talent acquisition, and cost accounting helps me build software that makes sense operationally, not just syntactically.

Lately, I've been fascinated by low-latency real-time systems. That led me to create Lilly Voice (a streaming AI voice assistant with dynamic interruption handling using FastAPI and WebSockets) and CodeChat-IQ (a synchronized collaborative pair-programming room powered by WebRTC).
